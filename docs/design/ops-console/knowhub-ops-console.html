Target operating model

Approved / Current

Decision

The programme will operate a federated service model. Domain teams retain delivery ownership while the Platform Enablement function owns shared controls, service standards and cross-domain evidence.

Context

Release 03 introduces shared identity, event and reporting capabilities. A single central team would create a delivery bottleneck; a fully decentralised model would fragment standards and assurance.

Decision effective from 01 September. All new system records must identify both a domain owner and a platform control owner.

Operating principles

  • Ownership remains closest to the service outcome.
  • Shared controls are published once and linked by reference.
  • Delivery evidence is captured through the workspace rather than separate reporting packs.

Acceptance

Approved by the programme board on 2026-08-18. Review interval: 90 days.

Target operating model

Approved / Historical

Decision

The programme will operate a federated service model. Domain teams retain delivery ownership while the Platform Enablement function owns shared controls and service standards.

Context

Release 03 introduces shared identity, event and reporting capabilities. The operating model must preserve domain accountability while avoiding duplicated assurance effort.

Operating principles

  • Ownership remains closest to the service outcome.
  • Shared controls are published once and linked by reference.
  • Evidence ownership is assigned during delivery planning.

Acceptance

Approved for board circulation on 2026-08-12.
